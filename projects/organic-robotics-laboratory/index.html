Overview

Research in mechanical testing, optical instrumentation and laboratory automation.

01 / VAM mechanical test bed

Designed a modular fixture integrating stepper and servo actuation with optical measurement boards. The system supports controlled torsion, bending and compression, with custom CAD housings and embedded controls.

02 / Thorlabs rotation stage automation

Worked on LabVIEW control of a Thorlabs PRM1ZB motorized rotation stage using the APT motor-control interface, exploring absolute angular positioning and event-driven control for research automation.

Research context

I joined Professor Robert Shepherd’s Organic Robotics Laboratory in January 2025 and continued the work through senior design and graduate study. My work has included volumetric additive manufacturing (VAM), resin preparation consistency, projector collimation and CAD aids for mixing and rotating hardware.

VAM / Mechanical architecture

I developed a modular test bed for characterizing printed specimens through torsion, bending and compression. Two circular PCBs carry three blue LEDs and three phototransistors, respectively. Custom adapters and rigid lids maintain optical alignment while the specimen is deformed. The design evolved from a bending-only fixture to a modular assembly with detachable components and improved clearances.

VAM / Actuation and controls

The compression mechanism uses a 28BYJ-48 stepper motor with a ULN2003 driver and gear train; torsion uses an MG995 servo and gearing. The intended motion ranges are 0–360° torsion, 0–90° bending and up to 0.5 in compression. An Arduino-based joystick and button interface selects one motor at a time and controls direction, speed and travel limits. The three phototransistors provide optical measurements during loading.

Thorlabs / Software integration

I worked with a PRM1ZB motorized rotation stage and the 32-bit APT motor-control library in LabVIEW. The workflow involved event-driven commands, absolute angular positioning and distinguishing position commands from velocity commands. I investigated library compatibility and the SetAbsMovePos/MoveAbsolute interface while developing the control application.

Deliverables and status

My VAM work produced Fusion 360 assemblies, electronics integration and Arduino control code for a tested prototype. The rotation-stage work focused on developing and debugging the control interface; final positioning accuracy and repeatability are not reported here.
