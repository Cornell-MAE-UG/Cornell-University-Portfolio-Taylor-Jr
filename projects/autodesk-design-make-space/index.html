Overview

Created instructional videos, scripts and machine demonstrations for mills, lathes, bandsaws, programmable machines and a four-axis mini-mill. Collaborated on safety documentation and trained students in manufacturing practices.

Role and scope

I worked as a Manufacturing Training & Documentation Assistant during the summers of 2025 and 2026 and continued during the Fall 2026 semester. My work combines hands-on machining, technical communication and student training.

Equipment and technical instruction

I developed scripts, demonstrations and instructional videos for conventional mills, lathes and bandsaws, as well as a TRAK lathe, programmable mill and four-axis mini-mill CNC. The training covers machine setup, safe operation and practical fabrication workflows.

Documentation workflow

Instructional materials translate shop procedures into clear sequences that students can follow. I collaborated on safety documentation and demonstrations, emphasizing safe machine operation alongside manufacturing fundamentals.

Impact

I trained more than 100 Cornell engineering students in manufacturing practices. This experience strengthened my ability to communicate technical procedures, design for manufacturability and connect CAD decisions to real machining constraints.
